Fun AI Projects

HTML Slide Research talks as web pages GitHub
  • Advantage: the animations of PowerPoint and the math of Beamer, in one web page that runs in any browser.
  • New key feature: comments.
    • Coauthors open one link with a passcode (no account, nothing to install). On any slide they press c, type a comment, draw a box on the exact spot and attach up to four screenshots. They can also edit the speaker script in the browser.
    • Every comment is tagged with its exact slide and click, so nothing is ambiguous.
    • AI agents (e.g., Claude and Codex) pick up new comments, fix the slide, check every click, republish and reply under the comment.
